Five amendments since it was proposed

Each one is the code catching up with something the household ran into. The ADR's §7 has the full text.

A1Oct 4 · 5 · 11

Reading rules, and a brake for wide asks

The first morning in serve mode answered seven of eight asks with nothing. Code now settles what the household's words make plain: a follow-up keeps the ask, no one named means the usual party.

A2Oct 14 · 15

Publisher feeds, by the owner's decision

Twenty event calendars and four food feeds are read even where robots.txt asks readers out. No mirrors, caches or proxies; failures show in the daily health line.

A3Oct 15

Research only when something is to be found

A save, a verdict or a question runs without the network, in two rounds. “Search”, “use Gemini” or “something new” always widens the hunt.

A4Oct 17

One brain, one memory

/coffee asked “where are you?” from an old trip's tables. Now an ask goes to the harness as words, and a lookup reads the harness's own memory.

A5Oct 17

Where you are

A location shared in Telegram, or pushed by the phone, is where every ask ranks from for three hours. A town said alone moves it too.

What the judge sees, and the say it has to earn

The judge can't be talked into agreeing, because it never sees the work: it gets the inputs and the answer, nothing in between, and it may not introduce a fact. Its say over a reply grows only as its verdicts match what the household actually said.

The knowledge base

One record per physical place, with two kinds of truth around it: what the web says, every claim with its source and read time, and what the household says, every save and verdict with who said it and when. Facts are never overwritten; a new reading is a new row, and the newest dated source wins.

Who owns what

Three kinds of code, three speeds of change. The control plane governs every agent and changes rarely. The harness runs one message and is built once. A tenant supplies data and tools, and is where product work happens week to week.

Control plane

core/ · new_plane/ · shared
  • Cost ledger: a row per model call, search, fetch and judge call; /cost.
  • Traces: one id per message, a span per step, a replay viewer.
  • Judge service: one call shape and calibration; checklists come from tenants.
  • Eval runner: outcome-scored replay from recorded calls, every night.
  • Weekly command screener: proposes commands; the owner approves.

Harness

core/harness/ · built once
  • Inbound: exact commands, then the model; lookups and asks.
  • Frame and contract schemas with bounded retries.
  • One model wire: tools, structured output, search, recorded calls.
  • Verifier, controller, renderer, recorder.
  • Scoped state and the time and place primitives.

Tenant: Bourdain
